### Project Description
AATTAM is a useless-project hackathon web app that scores how chaotically you shake your phone and automatically captures a hilariously blurry photo when you stop.
It then gives you a random Manglish roast and adds your score to a live leaderboard for everyone playing.


### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People have phones that are far too stable, and apparently need a way to prove how badly they can shake them.
AATTAM solves this completely unnecessary problem by turning random phone shaking into a competitive sport.


### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
AATTAM turns phone shaking into a ridiculous competition. Shake your phone as chaotically as possible, get a score, receive a roast, and see how you rank on the live leaderboard.
